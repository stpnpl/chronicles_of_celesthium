# Gameplay and combat

Talk to Riko to unlock travel. Clear the Rift Hound, Shardback Boar and Bonebound Sentinel at the Fractured Approach, and the Mirage Stalker and Voidborn Marauder at the Mirage Approach. You may choose the order of the encounters and approaches. Visit Yorikai and then the Guardian. The optional quest begins with Echo: collect the relay shard and return to her. It yields a restorative and an ending acknowledgement. The shard can be collected before accepting the quest.

The active champion has stability (health), energy and restoratives. Every successful action consumes a turn. Attack costs no energy. Guard halves the next enemy attack and restores up to 15 energy. Restoratives heal up to 45 stability. Disabled abilities cannot be used without energy. Victory restores up to 20 stability and energy; the refuge lantern restores both fully. There is no XP or grinding; route completion, rewards and the developing story provide progression.

- Kael: Stasis Pulse delays an enemy turn; Chrono Anchor adds a barrier; Rewind Cascade damages and heals.
- Nyra: Soulrend Riposte counters the next surviving hit; Vindicating Slash heals; Cursed Redemption trades 10 stability (cannot self-defeat) for high damage.
- Shoryu: Thunder Veil adds a barrier and charges a later attack; Froststep weakens the next enemy hit; Primal Convergence consumes the charge for extra damage.

The Guardian cycles between a telegraphed Rift Break, an exposed phase where attacks gain 8 damage, and a measured strike. A delayed enemy attack still advances its pattern. Barriers absorb damage once; Guard is consumed by the next attack. On defeat, Retry restores the player and flags to the beginning of the encounter, without persistence across refreshes. Return to Refuge restores the same checkpoint and lets the player rest before trying again.

## Enemy registry and friendly creatures

| Encounter | Integrity | Base attack | Pattern and role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Training Construct | 52 | 7 | Riko’s sparring automaton; alternating normal and heavier strikes. |
| Rift Hound | 65 | 9 | Fractured Approach; swift double strike adds 5 on alternating turns. |
| Shardback Boar | 78 | 9 | Fractured Approach; braces, halves the next strike and charges for +9, then attacks normally. |
| Bonebound Sentinel | 72 | 9 | Fractured Approach; braces, halves the next strike and counters it for 6, then attacks normally. |
| Mirage Stalker | 70 | 9 | Mirage Approach; builds a veil that halves the next turn’s damage, then attacks normally. |
| Voidborn Marauder | 74 | 10 | Mirage Approach; alternates a normal attack and a heavier +6 strike. |
| Void Rift Guardian | 150 | 12 | Threshold boss; Rift Break (+12), exposed phase (+8 to player damage), measured strike. |

Braced defenses are announced. Stasis can interrupt an enemy before it braces; in that case there is no next-turn defense or boar charge. Guard and barriers apply to enemy attacks; the sentinel's counter is a separate reaction to a damaging strike. Every fight can be retried independently. Winning restores up to 20 stability and energy; the refuge lantern remains available between encounters.

The friendly Temporal Anomaly points toward the relay and grants an 8-point barrier in the next Fractured Approach fight, once per run. The friendly Mirage Wisp reveals the solid lantern path, warns about the stalker's veil, and grants one restorative once per run. Both are optional episode additions, not established species histories. Their original artwork has been retained without modification.
