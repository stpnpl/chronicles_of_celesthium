# Celesthiumverse 100 — Universe & Timeline Mapping

This document outlines the planetary distribution and chronological placement of characters within the **Celesthiumverse 100** timeline.

---

## 1. Planet ABC: The Tech-Grit Era

**Year:** 3225 (Post-Cataclysm Era)
**Primary Realms:** Virelai, Draelmoor
**Theme:** High-tech survival, military grit, and temporal instability.

### Core Characters

* **Kael Vortex:** An elite super-soldier from Virelai utilizing a temporal-regulator to combat instability.
* **Nyra Khaine:** A rogue assassin from the militarized realm of Draelmoor seeking atonement.
* **Riko Blaze:** A street brawler from Virelai’s underbelly infused with volatile plasma energy.
* **Echo Rune:** A resonance specialist and former Iron Vow Syndicate operative weaponizing sonic frequencies.
* **Lira Valeska:** A high-tech aerial specialist from Virelai utilizing segmented flight systems.
* **Thorn Howl:** The apex predator of Draelmoor whose soul is bound to wolf spirits.
* **Korr Dravahn:** A psionic juggernaut contained within a heavy "Hollowed-Goliath" unit to prevent mental collapse.

---

## 2. Planet XYZ: The Mystic-Regal Era

**Year:** 12570 (Era of Peace / Legacy of the Sorcerer)
**Primary Realms:** Zantara, Obris Sanctum, Aetherion
**Theme:** Ancient traditions, psychic mastery, and elemental discipline.

### Core Characters

* **Shoryu Hayabusa (Lead):** Master of Storms and head of the Hayabusa Clan.
* **Hana Kurei:** The Regal Empress of Zantara who commands psychic winds and mirages.
* **Asoka the Unbroken:** A transcendental monk who has surrendered emotion to become a living mandala of psychic awareness.
* **Valeria Sol:** A Zantaran warrior who uses soul magic to manifest solar heat.
* **Neithara Sunspear:** The statuesque warrior-queen of Obris Sanctum who wields a solar javelin.
* **Kaen Ryuji:** A "Solar-Forge" monk who channels the heat of a dying sun.
* **Mei Soryana:** A martial artist who blends dancer-like grace with explosive fire energy.

---

## 3. Bridge Characters: Temporal Anomalies

These individuals are not bound to a single planet or timeline, often existing within the **Shattered Verge**, **Aetherion**, or the **Null Expanse**.

* **Veskara Nox:** A "Void-Knight" and survivor of a collapsed timeline who anchors herself within spatial fractures.
* **Xyla Thorne:** A high-ranking druid who navigates lawless temporal fractures to stabilize reality.
* **Borin Stoneheart:** The "Immovable Mountain" and chieftain from the Shattered Verge who acts as a shield for his people.
* **Zuriel Astralume:** A Wyrmguard sentinel from Aetherion who chronicles the movement of stars and timelines.
* **Yorikai:** An ancient warrior from a forgotten era who wields the Godforge Blade and stands as a timeless guardian.

---

## 4. Key Chronological Benchmarks

* **Year 0 (Planet ABC):** A global cataclysm occurs, nearly destroying the world and leading to the rise of Virelai's tech-dependence.
* **Year 0 (Planet XYZ):** A powerful individual establishes a lasting peace, founding the traditions followed by the Hayabusa and the Oracles.
* **The Convergence:** The point where the **Unmaker** begins devouring reality, forcing the timelines of 3225 and 12570 to overlap.
